Wesley Lobato Passos was born in Rio de Janeiro, Brazil, in 1991. He received his D.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from COPPE/UFRJ, Rio de Janeiro, RJ, Brazil, in 2024. Previously, he received his M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering also from COPPE/UFRJ in 2019, and his B.Sc. in Control Systems and Automation Engineering from the Federal Center for Technological Education (CEFET-RJ), Nova Iguaçu, RJ, Brazil, in 2016.

During his undergraduate studies, he participated in an academic exchange program at the University of Georgia (UGA), and took part in a summer research project at the Illinois Institute of Technology (IIT), both in the United States, through the Brazilian exchange program Science Without Borders from 2014 to 2015.

He received the Google Latin American Research Award in 2019 and 2020 for the project on detecting potential Aedes aegypti mosquito breeding grounds using computer vision and machine learning techniques.

Since 2024 he is a Professor with the Control Systems and Automation Engineering Department, and since 2025 a collaborating professor in the Graduate Program in Electrical Engineering (PPEEL), both at the CEFET-RJ. He is also a postdoctoral researcher with the Systems and Computer Engineering Program (PESC), COPPE/UFRJ. He is a member of the Institute of Electric and Electronic Engineers (IEEE): IEEE Circuits and Systems Society (CASS), IEEE Signal Processing Society (SPS), IEEE Young Professionals (YP), and the Brazilian Telecommunication Society (SBrT).

He has a background in Instrumentation, Control, and Industrial Automation projects. His research interests include Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and Signal Processing.
